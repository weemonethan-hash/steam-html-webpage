Welcome

Let's get you started

Steam Injector lets you browse Steam, spot a game's App ID automatically, and add it to Steam with one click. This short tour covers everything in 5 quick steps.

STEAM PATH
C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam
Auto DetectBrowse
Step 1

Tell it where Steam lives

In Configuration, click Auto Detect and the app will look for your Steam folder. If it can't find it, click Browse and pick the folder yourself. The path appears in the box once it's set.

store.steampowered.com
Step 2

Open a game's page

Use the built-in browser on the right. Pick Steam Store or SteamDB from the dropdown, search for a game and open its page. The back, forward, refresh and home buttons work just like a normal browser.

STEAMOnline
APP ID730
Step 3

The App ID shows up by itself

As soon as you're on a game page, its App ID appears in the Status card, and the Add to Steam button switches on. The Steam light turns green when Steam is running.

Add to Steam
Added!
Step 4

Click “Add to Steam”

Press Add to Steam in the Actions card. A progress bar slides up at the bottom of the browser while it works. Wait for it to finish before moving on.

Step 5

Restart Steam — you're done!

Click Restart Steam so it picks up the change. When Steam reopens, the game will be there. Repeat from step 2 any time you want to add another one.

“Add to Steam” is greyed out

It turns on once an App ID is detected. Open a specific game's page (not the home or search page) in the browser panel and check that an ID appears in the Status card.

Steam isn't detected

Click Browse and choose your Steam install folder, the one that contains the Steam program itself. Then check the Steam light in Status.

The game isn't in my library yet

Use Restart Steam. Steam only picks up changes when it starts, so it needs a fresh launch.
