Welcome

Let's get you started

Steam Injector lets you browse Steam, spot a game's App ID automatically, and add it to Steam with one click. This short tour covers everything in 5 quick steps.

steam.exe
Step 1 of 4

Configure Steam Path

Make sure Steam Injector knows where your Steam installation folder is located. Click Auto Detect or use Browse to select the folder containing steam.exe.

App ID: 123450
Step 2 of 4

Browse Steam & SteamDB

Use the built-in browser panel to navigate to Steam Store or SteamDB. The app automatically detects any game's App ID as you browse.

Add to Steam
Step 3 of 4

Click 'Add to Steam'

Once an App ID is recognized, the Add to Steam button becomes active. Click it to fetch and install the required manifest and plugin files automatically.

Restart Steam
Step 4 of 4

Restart Steam

After adding your games, click Restart Steam to apply changes. Your added items will now appear properly in your Steam library!

App ID isn't detecting automatically?

Try refreshing the page or navigating directly to the game's official store page or SteamDB entry. You can also manually drag and drop custom files into the designated drop zone.

Where do the files get installed?

Files are securely placed into your configured Steam installation directory under depotcache and config/stplug-in.

What if server downloads fail?

The app will automatically generate local template files using the standard template definitions so you can proceed without interruption.
